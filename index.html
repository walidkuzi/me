01 / Profile

The infrastructure layer of AI, designed end-to-end.

I'm a software engineer and founder specialized in building the infrastructure layer of AI — the platforms, orchestration engines, and retrieval architectures that make large language models do useful work inside real businesses. My focus sits at the intersection of LLM application architecture, distributed systems, and product strategy.

I design platforms end-to-end — schema, services, agent orchestration, retrieval pipelines, multi-tenant billing, and the UX that wraps all of it. I lead projects from zero to one: setting technical direction, coordinating teams, and shipping production-grade systems. Most of my current work targets SMB and enterprise markets across MENA, where I'm building vertical-specialized AI platforms.
