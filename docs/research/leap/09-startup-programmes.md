## What is open — and worth more than any of the above

### NTDP Relocate — up to $1.4m, non-dilutive · **the single biggest prize at LEAP for you**

The National Technology Development Program is Saudi Arabia's tech-sector funding arm, with
a **SAR 2.5bn budget** and **$430m+ committed to six new initiatives**. Its **Relocate**
programme offers, per company:

- up to **$235k** office space
- up to **$960k** local hiring support
- up to **$240k** relocation grant

**Stated preference is Series A+ or multinationals**, with the programme targeting 50
semiconductor-design firms and **20,000 AI professionals by 2030**. Precedents: **Movandi**
(US) was selected for Relocate; **Kneron** landed a **$2m** Saudi expansion grant.

NTDP also runs four financial products — **Fuel, LendTech, Loan Guarantee, Venture Debt** —
and the **NEXT ERA**, **MVPLab** and **Saudi Unicorns** programmes, plus a partnership with
**gener8tor** for a Riyadh investment accelerator. NTDP claims involvement with 800
companies and seven unicorns.

**They man a booth with programme managers who can act.** Go early on Day 1.

**The question to ask:** *"For Relocate, is Series A a hard bar, or is a signed Saudi
enterprise contract an acceptable substitute? And if we're below the bar today, is there a
Fuel or MVPLab route now with Relocate in twelve months?"*

### The MISA licence — and the Entrepreneur route that actually fits you

The new **Investment Law** (Royal Decree 2024, in force 2025) permits **100% foreign
ownership** in most sectors; software and AI are unrestricted.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Application fee** | **SAR 2,000** *(some sources say waived — verify)* |
| **Annual renewal** | SAR 10,000–60,000 depending on licence type; services at the low end |
| **Timeline** | **15–22 business days** for a well-prepared file; +5–10 days if document legalisation goes wrong |
| **Documents** | Parent commercial registration (legalised and attested), shareholder ID, and **audited financials for the most recent fiscal year by an internationally recognised audit firm** |

> **The audited-financials requirement is the real blocker for a young startup.**
>
> **The way around it: the Entrepreneur Licence** — a MISA category for early-stage
> innovative startups, which waives the trading-history requirement but **requires a letter
> of support from a MISA-recognised incubator, accelerator or VC.**
>
> **This is a concrete, tactical reason to talk to Saudi accelerators and funds at LEAP:
> their support letter is your licence.** Ask 500 Global, Sanabil, Merak, Nama, Monsha'at
> and NTDP directly whether they issue them and what they need to see.

**After MISA you still need:** Commercial Registration, Chamber of Commerce, GOSI, ZATCA,
Qiwa/Muqeem, a **physical local address**, and a corporate bank account — for which **the
authorised signatory must be physically present in Saudi Arabia.** Plan a second trip.

**Do not start any of this during LEAP week.** It is all doable remotely afterwards, and
spending capital on entity setup before you have a single pipeline conversation is
premature. Start it within two weeks *if* you got real traction.
