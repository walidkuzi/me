# Outreach templates

**Channel priority in Saudi Arabia: WhatsApp > LinkedIn DM > email.** WhatsApp is the
dominant business channel and gets answered on evenings and weekends. Cold email to a Saudi
enterprise inbox at 48 hours' notice is close to useless.

**Send on Sunday 30 August, Riyadh morning.** The Saudi weekend is Friday–Saturday, so
Sunday is the working day your message will actually be read.

**Target volume: 60–80 messages.** Expect 10–15% response and 6–10 meetings.

---

## 1 · Exhibitor decision-maker — LinkedIn DM or WhatsApp

> Hi [Name] — I see [Company] is at LEAP this week (stand [X]).
>
> I run [Your Company]. We build multi-agent AI systems — teams of AI agents that run a
> whole function end to end (support, procurement ops, claims) in Arabic and English,
> deployable in-Kingdom for PDPL and NCA CCC.
>
> Given [specific public fact], I'd value 15 minutes at your stand to show you a live agent
> running, not a deck.
>
> I'm on the floor Mon–Thu. Would Tuesday around 14:00 work, or is there a better window?
>
> — [Name], [Title], [Company] | [WhatsApp number]

**Why it works:** short; names their stand; cites a public fact; offers a demo rather than a
pitch; proposes a *specific* time; gives a WhatsApp number.

**The [specific public fact] is the whole message.** Never send this without one:

| Company | Use this |
|---|---|
| Riyad Bank | *"the Center of Intelligence work you've published — and that you've said agentic AI is the next horizon"* |
| Zain KSA | *"your AI Center of Excellence launch this year"* |
| stc | *"the 75 strategic AI agreements you signed in 2025"* |
| Elm | *"bringing Thiqah's service portfolio onto a common automation layer"* |

## 2 · Warm intro request — **the highest-yield message you will send**

> [Name] — quick ask. I'm at LEAP in Riyadh Mon–Thu building our Saudi pipeline.
>
> I noticed you're connected to [Target Person] at [Target Company]. Would you be
> comfortable making an intro? One line is plenty — happy to draft it.
>
> Context if useful: we build multi-agent AI systems for enterprises and I'd like 15
> minutes with them at their stand.
>
> No worries at all if not.

**Send 20 of these, not 5.** Saudi business culture strongly favours doing business with
people who come recommended — it is broadly understood that you need to know a key person
in an organisation and receive a personal introduction. **One warm intro is worth roughly
ten cold DMs here.**

## 3 · Investor

> Hi [Name] — I'm [Name], founder of [Company]. We're an AI agent platform: organisations
> compose "AI departments" — multi-agent teams that own a business function end to end.
>
> [One traction line: ARR, logos, or growth rate.]
>
> I'm in Riyadh for LEAP Mon–Thu and building our Saudi go-to-market — sovereign
> deployment, Arabic-first, targeting the government-adjacent SI channel (Elm, stc, SITE).
>
> Would you have 20 minutes on the floor or over coffee this week?

**That third paragraph is the one that matters.** Saudi funds want to see KSA traction and
a market-entry plan, not a generic global story. See `08-investor-map.md`.

**Send to:** STV, Sanabil, SVC, Raed, Impact46, Merak, Nama, Wa'ed Ventures, 500 Global
MENA, Khwarizmi, stc Ventures, ZAINIAC, Vision Ventures, Hala.

## 4 · Government programme desk — NTDP, Monsha'at, SVC, MISA

Different register: you are asking for guidance, not selling.

> As-salaamu alaykum [Name] — I'm [Name], founder of [Company]. We build AI agent teams for
> enterprises and we're looking seriously at establishing in Riyadh.
>
> I'll be at LEAP Mon–Thu and would like to understand whether [Relocate / Fuel / your
> co-investment programme] fits a company at our stage, and what evidence you'd need to see.
>
> Who owns applications, and are they on the stand this week?

## 5 · Post-meeting, same day — WhatsApp, **before you sleep**

> Great to meet you at [stand] today — [the specific thing you discussed]. I'll send [the
> specific thing] tomorrow morning.

**One line. Send it the same night.** Response rates collapse after 24 hours, and in Saudi
Arabia the WhatsApp message beats the email.

---

## 6 · Next-morning substantive follow-up

**Not a deck. A one-page written proposal for one specific agent, with a scope, a timeline
and a price.** Saudi buyers respond to specificity.

> [Name] — as promised.
>
> **What we'd build:** [one agent or department, named. e.g. "an AI credit-operations
> agent that takes an SME lending application, validates documents, checks policy and
> drafts the credit memo."]
>
> **Scope:** one process, [N] weeks.
> **Success metric:** [one number. e.g. "cycle time from 6 days to under 1."]
> **Price:** [number].
> **Deployment:** in-Kingdom, [HUMAIN cloud / center3 / your tenancy]. PDPL processor
> agreement included; NCA CCC control mapping attached.
>
> If the metric doesn't move, we stop. Shall I send a scope document?

Send it **before the floor opens** — if the 13:00 opening holds, you have 09:00–12:00 to
write.

## 7 · The close, before you leave Riyadh

> [Name] — heading out of Riyadh tomorrow. Three things:
>
> 1. [The agreed next step], by [date].
> 2. My WhatsApp is this number — easiest way to reach me.
> 3. **I'll be back in Riyadh the week of [date].** Would like to continue this in person.

**"I'll be back on [date]" converts far better than "let's stay in touch."** Relationship
precedes transaction here, and one trip is never enough. **Book the return before you fly
home.**

## Writing rules for this market

- **Lead with their fact, not your product.** No public fact, no message.
- **Never write "AI employees replacing headcount."** See `07-objections-and-context.md`.
- **Always propose a specific time**, never "let me know what works."
- **Always give a WhatsApp number**, never only an email.
- **Offer a live demo, not a deck** — 93% of LEAP sessions feature live demos.
- **Open with *As-salaamu alaykum* where it fits**, and keep the rest in English.
- **Short.** Every template here fits on a phone screen without scrolling. That is deliberate.
