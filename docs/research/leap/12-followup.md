# Follow-up and pipeline

**The show is not the work. The forty-eight hours after each conversation is the work.**

Most of what a founder collects at a 200,000-person event evaporates within a week, not
because the interest wasn't real but because nobody wrote down the next step while it was
still specific.

---

## The discipline, in order

### Same day, before you sleep

**WhatsApp every person you met, that night.** One line, naming the specific thing you
discussed. Templates in `11-outreach-templates.md`.

**Why that night:** response rates collapse after 24 hours, and by Wednesday your contact
has met eighty people. The message that arrives the same evening is remembered; the one
that arrives Friday is spam.

### Next morning, before the floor opens

If the reported **13:00 opening** holds, you have **09:00–12:00 free every day.** This is
the most valuable unstructured time of your week. Use it for the **substantive follow-up**:
a **one-page written proposal for one specific agent — scope, timeline, price**, not a deck.

**Never send a deck as a follow-up.** Saudi buyers respond to specificity, and a scoped
one-pager is a decision document. A deck is homework.

### Before you leave Riyadh

Every warm contact must have all three:

1. **A named next step with a date** — not "let's stay in touch"
2. **Your WhatsApp number**, and theirs written down
3. **A return commitment**: *"I'll be back in Riyadh the week of [date]"*

**Book the return trip before you fly home.** Relationship precedes transaction in this
market, and one trip is never enough. A named future date converts far better than an
open-ended intention.

### Within two weeks

- Start the **MISA Entrepreneur Licence** application *if* you got real traction — 15–22
  business days, and having an entity in progress materially changes the second meeting.
  See `09-startup-programmes.md`.
- Send the **first quarterly investor update** to everyone who said "too early." In a
  referral-driven ecosystem where nobody funds you from a hall meeting, the quarterly
  update is the thing that actually converts. Write it against the answer they gave to
  *"what would I need to show you in nine months?"*

## The tracker

One sheet. Fill a row the moment a conversation ends — not at the end of the day, when you
will have forgotten which of six people said what.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name / role / company** | |
| **WhatsApp** | **The only contact field that matters.** An email address without a number is a dead lead. |
| **Play** | CHANNEL / DIRECT / DESIGN PARTNER / INVESTOR / PROGRAMME |
| **Tier** | A / B / C |
| **What they actually said** | Verbatim where possible. "Budget sits with digital, not IT" is worth more than "seemed interested." |
| **The qualifying answer** | Did they have budget? Did they already buy a platform? |
| **Objection raised** | Residency / Arabic / incumbent / build-in-house / no entity |
| **Next step + date** | If this is blank, it isn't a lead. |
| **Same-day WhatsApp sent?** | ☐ |
| **One-pager sent?** | ☐ |
| **Return-visit date given?** | ☐ |

## Scoring your own show, honestly

**Target: 15–25 real WhatsApp numbers with a specific agreed next step.** Not 200 badge
scans — a scan puts you in a marketing nurture database, not a pipeline.

Realistic shape of a well-run four days *(calibrated inference)*:

- **25–40** substantive conversations
- of which **8–12** are investor conversations
- of which **2–4** produce a follow-up call
- **Term sheets originating at a conference: effectively zero.** Nobody funds you from a
  hall meeting.

### What actually counts as a successful LEAP

Judged three weeks later, not on the flight home:

1. **One named Saudi pilot** in motion — even unpaid, even at LOI stage.
2. **One non-dilutive path open** — an NTDP conversation with a follow-up booked.
3. **One ecosystem sponsor** — an accelerator, fund or programme willing to issue a **MISA
   Entrepreneur Licence support letter**.
4. **One channel conversation live** — Elm, stc, SITE or a Big 4 alliance lead who has
   asked for something specific.

**Any two of those four makes the trip worth it.** All four would be an exceptional show.

## The failure modes

- **Collecting instead of qualifying.** Two hundred scans and no next steps is a failed
  show that feels like a successful one.
- **Waiting to follow up until you're home.** By then you're one of eighty.
- **Sending a deck instead of a scope.**
- **"Let's stay in touch."** It means no. Convert it to a date or let it go.
- **Never coming back.** One trip reads as tourism. The second trip is when people start
  treating you as real.
