## The date trap — read this before you book anything

LEAP has historically been a **February** event (2022: 1–3 Feb; 2025: 9–12 Feb). For 2026
it was first scheduled for **13–16 April 2026**, then **postponed to 31 August –
3 September 2026** because of the regional conflict involving Iran, the US and Israel,
which disrupted travel and logistics across Gulf events.

**Stale "13–16 April 2026" pages are still live**, including on LEAP's own site and across
third-party listing sites. If you see April, you are looking at an abandoned schedule.

> **31 Aug – 3 Sep 2026 is correct.** February is the old normal, April was cancelled.

### What the big numbers actually mean

Treat the headline AUM and investment figures with care — they are real, and they are
mostly not about you.

| Edition | Headline | What it actually was |
|---|---|---|
| LEAP 2024 | $11.9bn → restated $13.4bn | AWS $5.3bn cloud region; ServiceNow $500m; Investcorp-led $500m growth fund |
| LEAP 2025 | **$14.9bn**, announced day one | Groq + Aramco Digital $1.5bn; ALAT + Lenovo $2bn; KKR + Gulf Data Hub; Salesforce $500m; Tencent Cloud $150m; Alfanar $1.4bn |
| Cumulative | **$42.4bn+** since LEAP began | — |

Roughly **95% of that is data centres, chips, cloud regions and corporate capex.** The
startup-relevant slice of LEAP 2025's $14.9bn was about **$365m of *fund formation*** —
money that becomes deployable over three to five years, not cheques written at the show.

Likewise, the **$14.3 trillion AUM** figure is dominated by J.P. Morgan-, Morgan
Stanley- and General Atlantic-class institutions that will never write a pre-seed cheque.
The number of investors present who could realistically fund an early-stage AI agent
company is closer to **100–200**.

> **Do not go to LEAP believing $14.9bn is in the room for you. Go because the *buying*
> budget is real and reachable by a small company in a way a Series A is not.**

## DeepFest — your actual habitat

**DeepFest is LEAP's dedicated AI programme, co-located inside the main event, same
dates, same venue, and covered by the same pass.** Do not buy a separate ticket.

2026 first-time DeepFest exhibitors and sponsors include **Google Cloud, AMD, Blaize,
Cerebras, Supermicro, Salesforce, EY and Xerox**.

Anyone selling AI agents should treat the DeepFest floor and stages as home base rather
than general LEAP.

## What does not exist here

Worth knowing so you don't plan around them:

- **No "Birds of a Feather" format and no hosted-buyer programme** under those names.
  LEAP's structured formats are pre-scheduled 1:1s, LEAP Connect, Ecosystem Xchange, and
  closed-door LP/GP sessions on the Investor Pass.
- **No published side-event list.** They exist; they are simply not indexed. See
  `10-attendance-playbook.md` for how to find them.
