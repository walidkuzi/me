# LEAP 2026 — field dossier

> **Riyadh Exhibition & Convention Centre, Malham · 31 August – 3 September 2026**
> Compiled 29 August 2026 — two days before the doors open.

A working dossier for attending LEAP 2026 as the founder of an **AI agent-builder
platform** — a product that builds AI employees, AI departments and whole AI teams.
It answers three questions: *who do I sell to*, *what do I sell them*, and *what else
can I get out of this week*.

This is a field manual, not an essay. It is written to be read on a phone in a taxi to
Malham.

## Read this first

Three findings reframe the whole trip. They are argued in full inside, but if you read
nothing else:

1. **Rocket Fuel is closed.** The 100 startups were selected from 3,000+ applications
   months ago. You cannot enter. Attend the stage as audience — the investors sitting
   around you are the point.
2. **Chase pilots, not capital.** Saudi VC funding fell **74% year-on-year in H1 2026**
   ($219m across 72 deals, no late-stage rounds at all), and 67% of what did move went
   to fintech. Meanwhile corporate and sovereign *buying* budgets are enormous. The
   money in the room is customer money.
3. **The single highest-value stop is NTDP** — up to **$1.4m non-dilutive** via the
   Relocate programme. Not a VC. A government booth with staff who can act.

## A word on reliability

`onegiantleap.com` was unreachable from the environment this was compiled in, so the
official exhibitor directory, floor plan and live ticket prices could **not** be read
first-hand. Everything here is built from press coverage, official statements quoted in
third-party reporting, and the known Saudi enterprise landscape.

Every claim is marked **confirmed**, **inferred** or **unverified** in `sources.md`, and
`10-attendance-playbook.md` opens with a short list of things to check yourself before
you pay for anything. Trust the structure; verify the numbers.
