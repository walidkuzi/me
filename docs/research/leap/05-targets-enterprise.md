## Telco — the three-horse race you can exploit

### 1 · stc Group / solutions by stc / center3 — `CHANNEL + DIRECT` · Tier A

**What they are.** The largest telco in the Kingdom, plus its enterprise IT arm
(**solutions by stc**) and its data-centre and sovereign-cloud arm (**center3**, which
holds 49% of the JV with HUMAIN targeting up to 1GW). **stc sponsors LEAP 2026's free
General Admission passes** — they will be impossible to miss on the floor.

**Why they fit.** **75 strategic AI agreements signed in 2025.** A partnership with
**Cohere** for enterprise generative AI. Using **Oracle Alloy** to become a sovereign
cloud provider. They are building enormous compute capacity and **need applications to
sell on top of it.** You are an application.

**The pain.** A massive B2C contact-centre cost base. Enterprise customers asking "what do
I actually run on your sovereign cloud?" The center3 JV needs demand-side workloads.

**Pitch — two levels, and the second is the real one.**
- *Level 1 (the proof point):* an Arabic AI care agent for stc consumer support — billing,
  SIM, roaming, fault triage.
- *Level 2 (the prize):* **your platform packaged as an "AI Employees" SKU, sold by
  solutions by stc into their enterprise base, hosted on center3.** Ask for a co-sell
  pilot with one named stc enterprise account.

**Buyer.** VP Enterprise Business / solutions by stc GM · center3 commercial lead · stc
Group Chief AI or Chief Digital Officer.

### 2 · Zain KSA — `DIRECT + CHANNEL` · Tier A · **the fastest mover of the three**

**Why they punch above their market share.** Zain is #3 by size but **the most publicly
committed to agentic AI**. They launched an **AI Center of Excellence in 2026, explicitly
framed against the Year of AI**, and are moving to an **"AI-first operating model"** under
Zain Group's *"4WARD – Progress with Purpose"* strategy. They have a **Cisco partnership**
for AI infrastructure and GPU-as-a-Service. And **ZAINIAC, their venture arm, has already
invested in Actly** — an AI startup doing personalised CX and decision intelligence *via
AI agents*.

> **That last fact is the tell: they have already bought this category once.** The
> category-education work is done. And as the challenger, they move faster than stc.

**The pain.** Cost-to-serve against stc's scale. Enterprise differentiation. **A brand-new
Center of Excellence with a mandate and nothing shipped yet.**

**Pitch.** *"Give your new AI CoE its first three shipped AI employees in 90 days"* — an
Arabic care agent, a B2B order-to-activation agent, and an internal IT helpdesk agent. A
new CoE needs early visible wins more than it needs a strategy deck.

**Buyer.** Head of the AI Center of Excellence · Chief Enterprise Business Officer ·
ZAINIAC (who may also invest).

### 3 · Mobily — `DIRECT` · Tier C · the gap play

Strongest financial results in twelve years, a Nokia managed-services deal — and **the
weakest public agentic-AI signal of the three telcos.**

*This next part is inference, not sourced:* that silence probably means they are behind,
know it, and have **no incumbent AI vendor locked in**. That is a better position for you
than either competitor.

**Pitch framing.** *"Zain launched an AI Center of Excellence and stc signed 75 AI
agreements last year. What's Mobily's Year of AI story?"* Competitive pressure is a
legitimate and effective opener in this market — just deliver it without smugness.

**Buyer.** Chief Digital Officer · Chief Enterprise Officer.

## Energy and industry

### 4 · Aramco Digital — `DESIGN PARTNER` · Tier B

**What they have.** **METABRAIN** — a 250-billion-parameter industrial LLM trained on 90
years of proprietary data, targeting 1 trillion parameters. Plus the **Groq JV** building
what is billed as the world's largest inferencing data centre. IKTVA local content reached
**70% in early 2026**, targeting 75% by 2030.

**Why now.** Public analysis frames Aramco's 2026 challenge as the **"transition from
building infrastructure to commercial operation and monetization."** They have a
world-class industrial model and no obvious high-volume enterprise application layer on
top of it. **That gap is your pitch.**

**Pitch.** *"AI procurement analyst department for Aramco supply chain"* — agents that
read RFQs, score vendors against IKTVA local-content criteria, flag anomalies and draft
award recommendations. Second: **METABRAIN-grounded field-engineer agents** for
maintenance procedure lookup.

**Buyer.** Aramco Digital CEO office · VP Digital Products · Head of AI Platforms.

**Deal shape.** A design partnership building agents *on* METABRAIN and Groq. This is a
credibility deal more than a revenue deal — **the logo is worth more than the money.**
Note that **Wa'ed Ventures**, Aramco's $500m VC arm, is your parallel route in; see
`08-investor-map.md`.

Accenture or Deloitte as prime contractor.

## Aviation and healthcare

### 7 · Riyadh Air — `CHANNEL via IBM` · Tier B

PIF's new airline, flying commercially from H1 2026, and self-declared **"the world's
first AI-native airline."** IBM is coordinating **59 workstreams across 60+ technology
partners** (Adobe, Apple, FLYR, Microsoft), and is **already using watsonx Orchestrate to
build an agentic AI concierge for employees.**

> **⚠ IBM owns the prime relationship.** You are pitching to be one of the 60+ partners,
> not to displace IBM. Go *through* them, not around them.

**Pitch.** *"AI IRROPS department"* — on a delay or cancellation, agents proactively
rebook, notify, compensate and log, in Arabic and English, across GDS and CRM. Highly
measurable, and **outside IBM's employee-concierge scope**, which is what makes it sellable.

**Buyer.** Chief Digital & Technology Officer · VP Guest Experience · the IBM engagement lead.

### 8 · MOH / Seha Virtual Hospital, via Lean Business Services — `CHANNEL + SANDBOX` · Tier B

**The scale.** Seha Virtual Hospital connects **200+ hospitals** and handled **16m+
virtual appointments and consultations in 2025**. AI x-ray and breast-cancer detection has
been in production for over two years.

**The way in — and it is unusually clean.** The **Healthcare Innovation Sandbox** has
supported **342 solutions and graduated 25** as of July 2026. **That is a documented,
non-Etimad route into Saudi government healthcare for a foreign vendor** — rare enough to
be worth reorganising your week around.

**Pitch.** *"AI patient-navigation department"* — agents that triage inbound patient
contact in Arabic, book and reschedule virtual clinic slots, chase pre-appointment prep,
and escalate red-flag symptoms. Second: an **AI referral-coordination agent** between
peripheral hospitals and Seha specialists.

**Buyer.** Deputy Minister for Digital Health / MOH Digital Transformation GM · **Lean
Business Services** product leadership (the Yamamah platform team). Lean is PIF-backed and
is your delivery and hosting partner here.

**Action.** Apply to the Innovation Sandbox — it is the fastest legitimate entry — and
pitch Lean in parallel.

## The channel — where the leverage actually is

### 9 · The Big 4 — Deloitte first — `CHANNEL` · Tier A

**Why Deloitte leads.** The most concrete agentic assets in the region: a **Global Agentic
Network launched in the Middle East in June 2025**, a **Middle East Centre of Excellence
for Oracle AI Agents (October 2025)**, **$3bn** committed to generative AI through FY2030,
and the NVIDIA-powered **Zora AI** platform. They are **actively staffing Riyadh** ahead
of the 2026 hyperscaler data-centre launches and need product to deliver on.

**Then:** **Accenture** (AI Refinery, ~77,000 AI professionals; Nick Law of Accenture Song
speaks at LEAP 2026) · **PwC** (a **confirmed dedicated LEAP 2026 stand**, collaborating
on-stand with Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, Salesforce, Google Cloud and AWS — **go there**) ·
**KPMG** (Workbench; the KPMG–Uniphore alliance; co-authored *HFS Horizons: Agentic
Services 2026*) · **EY**.

**Why the category matters more than any single firm.** KPMG's own research found that
**39% of Saudi organisations commit $100m–$249.9m annually to digital technology, and 76%
expect measurable enterprise-scale AI ROI within 12 months — the highest confidence of any
region surveyed.** The Big 4 hold the delivery contracts for those budgets.

**Pitch.** *"You have the demand and the delivery bench. What you don't have is a
deployable multi-agent platform that satisfies PDPL, NCA CCC and in-Kingdom data
residency. We do. White-label it."*

**Buyer.** Middle East AI / agentic practice lead · Alliances & Ecosystem director ·
Riyadh Managing Partner (Technology).

**Deal shape.** Alliance agreement plus a first joint client pilot — revenue share, or
your platform under their statement of work.

**Qualify the exclusivity scope in the first five minutes.** If it is broad, thank them
and walk. That is a five-minute conversation, not a thirty-minute one.

**Buyer.** Head of Alliances · CTO.
