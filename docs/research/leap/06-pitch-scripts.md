# Booth scripts — what to actually say

Literal scripts. Read them aloud twice before you fly so they don't sound read.

Three rules behind every script below:

1. **Never open with your pitch to whoever greets you.** Open with a question about
   *their* product. They relax, and you learn who they are.
2. **End on a question.** A pitch that ends on a question becomes a conversation inside
   thirty seconds. A pitch that ends on a claim becomes a brochure hand-off.
3. **Never say "AI replaces employees."** See `07-objections-and-context.md` — this is the
   one phrase that can lose you a room in Saudi Arabia.

---

## The universal opener

Use this when you have no specific intelligence about the person in front of you.

> *"As-salaamu alaykum. I'm [Name], founder of [Company]. We build **AI teammates** — not
> chatbots. You compose an agent the way you'd brief an analyst: it has a role, it has
> tools, and it does the whole workflow end to end. It runs inside Saudi data residency
> and it works in Arabic. Can I ask you one question — [qualifying question]?"*

**Why it's built this way.** "AI teammates" is a category claim, not a feature claim.
"Not chatbots" pre-empts the Copilot objection before it's raised. Data residency and
Arabic pre-empt the two objections you will hit in literally every Saudi conversation. And
it ends on a question.

**The Arabic bookends.** Open with *"As-salaamu alaykum"* and close with *"Shukran,
tasharrafna"* (thank you, pleased to meet you). English is entirely sufficient at LEAP —
these two phrases are not about language, they're about signalling you did the homework.
Small effort, disproportionate return.

---

## Getting past junior booth staff

Most stands are fronted by marketing or graduate hires. This is a solved problem:

1. Ask about their product first. Genuinely listen to the answer.
2. Then: **"Who on your team owns AI agents or automation? I'd like to leave something
   specific with them."** Naming a *function* rather than a seniority gets a real answer;
   asking for "someone senior" gets a business card.
3. The line that works best: **"I don't want to take your time — is [Named Executive] here
   today, or should I come back at a specific hour?"** Using a name you pulled from
   LinkedIn moves you instantly from vendor to someone who did their homework.
4. Leave the **printed bilingual one-pager**, not a QR code. Everyone has a QR code.

## Per-target scripts

### Elm — `CHANNEL`

> *"As-salaamu alaykum. [Name], [Company] — we build multi-agent systems, teams of AI
> agents that run a whole service line end to end. I'm not here to sell you software. Elm
> has the Etimad registration, the clearances and the government relationships that we
> will never have. We have a platform that ships an Arabic citizen-service agent in weeks.
> Who owns partnerships or OEM at Elm?"*

**Qualifier:** *"Post-Thiqah, is there one automation layer across the combined service
portfolio, or is each service line still solving it separately?"*
**Objection — "we build in-house":** *"Of course you do — and you'll keep the customer
relationship and the data. What we sell is the eighteen months you don't spend building
orchestration, evaluation and guardrails. White-label it as Elm."*

### stc / solutions by stc — `CHANNEL`

> *"You signed 75 strategic AI agreements last year and you're building up to a gigawatt
> with center3. My question is what your enterprise customers actually *run* on it. We
> build AI departments — procurement, support, back office. I'd like to explore an 'AI
> Employees' SKU that solutions by stc sells and center3 hosts."*

**Qualifier:** *"Who decides what goes into the solutions by stc enterprise catalogue?"*
**Objection — "we partner with Cohere already":** *"Cohere gives you models. We're the
layer above — the department that uses them. We're model-agnostic, so Cohere or ALLaM
underneath is fine by us."*

### HUMAIN — `DESIGN PARTNER` · **read the warning in `03-targets-government.md` first**

> *"As-salaamu alaykum. [Name], [Company]. We build vertical AI departments — the domain
> agents that sit **on top of** an agentic OS. HUMAIN ONE is the platform; we build the AI
> procurement department, the AI HR-operations department, for specific industries. We're
> model-agnostic and we want to run on ALLaM. Who owns your ISV or partner ecosystem?"*

**Qualifier:** *"Is there a formal partner or marketplace programme with an application,
or is this bilateral right now?"*
**Never say:** anything that positions you as an alternative to HUMAIN ONE.

### Zain KSA — `DIRECT`

> *"Congratulations on the AI Center of Excellence — I saw it's explicitly tied to the
> Year of AI. My question is what it ships first. We build AI departments, and we can have
> three in production in ninety days: Arabic care, B2B order-to-activation, internal IT
> helpdesk. A new CoE needs early visible wins more than another strategy."*

**Qualifier:** *"What does the CoE have to demonstrate by the end of 2026?"*
**Warm angle:** ZAINIAC already invested in Actly — they have bought this category before.
Say so; it shortens the education.

### Riyad Bank — `DIRECT`

> *"You've said publicly that agentic AI is the next horizon for the Center of
> Intelligence. That's why I'm here. We build AI departments — for you that's credit
> operations: an agent team that takes an SME lending application, validates the
> documents, checks policy, drafts the credit memo, and hands your underwriter a
> decision-ready package. Ten-week pilot, one metric: cycle time."*

**Qualifier:** *"Is the Center of Intelligence buying platforms, or building on one it's
already chosen?"*
**Objection — "data residency":** answer before they finish the sentence. See
`07-objections-and-context.md`.

### Deloitte and the Big 4 — `CHANNEL`

> *"You have the demand and you have the delivery bench in Riyadh. What you don't have —
> and I'd love to be wrong — is a deployable multi-agent platform that already satisfies
> PDPL, NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls and in-Kingdom residency. That's us. White-label
> it under your SOW. Who runs the agentic practice for the Middle East?"*

**Qualifier:** *"On your current Saudi AI engagements, are you building the agent layer
yourself or integrating someone's?"*

### Fintech scale-ups — Tamara, Tabby, Foodics, Geidea — `DIRECT`

> *"Quick one — how many support tickets a month, and how fast is headcount growing
> against it? We build AI departments. For you that's merchant onboarding: document
> collection, KYB, exception handling, in Arabic and English. Two-week pilot, and if it
> doesn't move your containment rate you don't pay for year one."*

**Qualifier:** *"Who owns the support cost line — you or ops?"*
**Why this one is blunter:** these are English-speaking, VC-backed operators who buy
software the way you do. Skip the ceremony.

### Aramco Digital — `DESIGN PARTNER`

> *"METABRAIN is 250 billion parameters trained on ninety years of proprietary data, and
> the public read on 2026 is that the challenge is monetising the infrastructure you
> built. That needs an application layer. We build AI departments — procurement analysts
> that score vendors against IKTVA criteria, field-engineer agents grounded in METABRAIN.
> We'd build on your model, not around it."*

**Qualifier:** *"Is Aramco Digital looking for application partners on METABRAIN, or
building the app layer internally?"*

### SDAIA / DGA — `DESIGN PARTNER`

> *"I read DGA's paper on AI agents as government partners — the 'first-line digital
> clerk' framing. That's exactly what we build. I know we can't transact without a CR and
> Etimad registration, so I'm not here to sell. I'd like to understand what a reference
> implementation would need to look like to satisfy the AI Adoption Framework."*

**Why this works:** naming their own research, and openly acknowledging you can't
transact, both signal seriousness. Saudi counterparts respond well to a vendor who knows
the rules.

### Riyadh Air — `CHANNEL via IBM`

> *"You're building the first AI-native airline with IBM across 59 workstreams. I'm not
> here to displace watsonx Orchestrate — that's your employee concierge. I'm interested in
> IRROPS: when a flight cancels, agents that rebook, notify, compensate and log
> automatically, Arabic and English. Different scope, same architecture. Who runs the
> partner workstreams?"*

### Ejada — `CHANNEL, contested` · **a five-minute conversation**

> *"I know about the Dyna.Ai partnership for call centres. I'm not here for voice. Is that
> exclusivity scoped to contact centre, or does it cover back-office automation too?"*

If the answer is "everything" — thank them and walk. Don't spend thirty minutes finding out.

## The one qualifying question, by play type

| Play | The question that saves you twenty minutes |
|---|---|
| **CHANNEL** | *"Who owns partnerships, and have you signed an agent platform already?"* |
| **DIRECT** | *"Who owns the budget for this, and is there money allocated in 2026 or are we talking 2027?"* |
| **DESIGN PARTNER** | *"What would you need to see to make us a reference implementation?"* |

**If the answer is vague, leave.** With 200,000 people on the floor and four days, the
scarce resource is your attention, not their interest.

## Closing every conversation

Never end on "let's stay in touch." End on all three of these:

1. **A named next step with a date.** *"I'll send a one-page scope for the credit-ops
   pilot on Sunday."*
2. **A WhatsApp number** — theirs, written down. Not a badge scan. See `12-followup.md`.
3. **A return commitment.** *"I'll be back in Riyadh the week of [date]."* Relationship
   precedes transaction here, and one trip is never enough.
