# Targets — government, sovereign AI and giga-projects

The highest-leverage and slowest-moving accounts in the Kingdom. **You cannot transact
with a ministry this week** — government procurement runs through Etimad and needs a
commercial registration, and usually a Regional HQ licence. Come here for relationships,
reference architecture and channel access, and be explicit that that is what you want.
Saudi counterparts respect a vendor who knows the rules.

---

## 1 · Elm (+ Thiqah) — `CHANNEL / RESELLER` · Tier A · **your single best target**

**What they are.** Tadawul-listed digital-government operator. Runs **Absher** (the
national citizen and resident services gateway) and **Nusuk** (Hajj and Umrah). Revenue
~SAR 5.3bn, with 38.5% of 2024 revenue from government. Acquired **Thiqah** from PIF for
**SAR 3.4bn (~$907m)**, adding justice, real estate and corporate services.

**Why they fit.** They are *the* channel to Saudi government, and they cannot build every
agent themselves fast enough. 2026 is the Year of AI and every ministry is under an SDAIA
adoption mandate. Secondary reporting describes Elm entering a "full industrialization
phase, deploying advanced AI agents at scale" in 2026 — *treat as unverified, but ask
about it at the stand and watch the reaction.*

**The pain.** Enormous, seasonal, Arabic-language citizen-service volume across hundreds
of e-services. A fragmented post-Thiqah portfolio that needs a common automation layer.
Pressure to show AI outcomes before December.

**Pitch.** *"Tier-1 Arabic citizen-service agent for Absher"* — resolves the top 30
Absher/Muqeem transaction queries end to end in Saudi dialect, hard escalation to human
staff, full SDAIA-compliant audit trail. Second use case: a **Nusuk Hajj-season surge
agent** — seasonality makes the ROI arithmetic trivial.

**Buyer.** Chief Digital or Technology Officer · Head of AI & Emerging Tech · the Thiqah
integration lead.

**Deal shape.** Reseller or OEM. Elm white-labels your platform into government
contracts. Start with a paid POC on one service line, then a revenue-share agreement.
**Elm has the Etimad registration, the CR, the local-content score and the security
clearances you don't.** That is the entire point.

## 2 · HUMAIN — `DESIGN PARTNER` · Tier A · **handle with care**

**What they are.** PIF's commercial AI champion, chaired by the Crown Prince, CEO
**Tareq Amin** — a headline speaker at LEAP this week. Full stack: data centres, cloud,
models, applications. Compute partners include NVIDIA (up to 600k GPUs over three years),
AWS (a Riyadh "AI Zone", up to 150k GPUs), AMD (~$10bn), Qualcomm, xAI, and **center3**,
a JV that is 51% HUMAIN / 49% stc targeting up to 1GW.

> ### ⚠ They already ship your product
>
> **HUMAIN ONE**, launched at FII9 in Riyadh on **28 October 2025**, is a
> *"next-generation agentic AI operating system"* that unifies **HR, finance, procurement
> and productivity in a single language-based interface** — the user states intent and it
> executes across connected systems and agents. It runs on HUMAIN's agentic orchestration
> engine and **ALLaM**, the Arabic-first national LLM.
>
> Amin's own words: *"making AI an active partner that understands your goals, anticipates
> needs, and executes tasks autonomously."*
>
> **Do not walk into HUMAIN positioning as a competing agentic OS.** You will be
> dismissed, and the framing will follow you into every government and PIF-portfolio room
> afterwards.

**The opening.** HUMAIN ONE is an OS and an orchestration layer. It needs a **long tail
of domain agents and vertical departments** — HR operations for a hospital group,
procurement for a giga-project, claims for an insurer. They cannot build them all.

**Pitch.** *"We build the vertical AI departments that run on HUMAIN ONE and ALLaM."*
Bring **one** concrete, buildable department and show it running against ALLaM.

**Buyer.** Chief Product Officer · VP Applications · partnerships lead. **Amin himself is
unreachable this week** — find his product and partnership people at the stand.

**Deal shape.** Technology partnership, marketplace listing or OEM. Not a sale. Possibly
investment.

## 3 · SDAIA and DGA — `DESIGN PARTNER` · Tier B · strategic, no near-term revenue

**What they are.** **SDAIA** is the national data and AI authority *and* the PDPL
regulator; president **Abdullah bin Sharaf Alghamdi**. It built **ALLaM**, runs a National
Data Lake integrating **430 government systems**, published a mandatory **AI Adoption
Framework** for every public-sector entity, and drove the Cabinet's designation of 2026 as
the Year of AI. Vision 2030 target: AI contributing **SAR 74bn (~$19.7bn)** to GDP by 2030.

**DGA** (Digital Government Authority) regulates digital government across all ministries.

> ### They have already written your pitch deck
>
> DGA has published formal research titled **"AI Agents as Government Partners"** and
> **"AI Agents & Large Action Models in Digital Government."** Their own framing describes
> conversational agents as *"first-line digital clerks, resolving citizen queries
> instantly in Arabic and English and escalating only complex cases"* — and names
> **Absher, Tawakkalna and Najiz** as the platforms those agents should sit on.
>
> This is the strongest "the buyer has publicly described your product" artifact in the
> entire Saudi market. Read both papers before you fly, and quote them back.

**The pain.** SDAIA must show hundreds of entities actually *adopting*, not merely
complying, during the Year of AI. They need reference implementations.

**Pitch.** *"A reference AI-clerk implementation for one ministry service, built on
ALLaM, deployed in-Kingdom, with a full AI-Adoption-Framework compliance pack."*

**What you want out of it.** No revenue. (a) A named reference deployment, (b) inclusion
in whatever approved-vendor or adoption catalogue exists, (c) an ALLaM integration.
**Highest strategic leverage in the market, slowest clock.**

## 4 · SITE (Saudi Information Technology Company) — `CHANNEL` · Tier B

**What they are.** PIF-owned, and the **National Cybersecurity Authority's strategic and
technical partner** — credited with Saudi Arabia's Tier 1 "Role-modelling" ranking in the
ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2026. Signed into a November 2025 MoU with **Microsoft and
PIF on sovereign cloud services**.

**Why they matter.** If you want to sell agents into anything security-classified or
sovereign, SITE is simultaneously the gatekeeper and the reseller. **A SITE relationship
functions as a de facto NCA credential.**

**Pitch.** *"AI SOC analyst department"* — tier-1 alert triage, enrichment and ticketing
agents, deployable on sovereign infrastructure under NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls.

**Buyer.** CTO · Head of Managed Security Services.

## 5 · Alat — `PARTNERSHIP / INVESTMENT` · Tier C · not a customer

PIF's industrial and technology manufacturing vehicle, with a $100bn ambition. A **$2bn
Lenovo partnership**, a SoftBank robotics JV, nine advanced manufacturing units including
AI & Digital Hardware, and a **40,000 sqm AI-integrated "Future Hub" in Riyadh**.

**The play.** Alat *invests in and localises* AI companies. It is how a foreign AI company
acquires a manufactured-in-Saudi, PIF-blessed identity. Talk to corporate development and
partnerships, not to a buyer.

## 6 · Giga-projects — NEOM, ROSHN, Diriyah, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global, NHC, New Murabba — `DIRECT` · Tier C

All in heavy execution and award mode through 2026. NEOM's **DataVolt** AI data centre
campus is breaking ground. Diriyah awarded a SAR 717m contract in a single announcement;
ROSHN is awarding SAR 262m residential packages.

**The pain.** Extreme back-office volume against deliberately lean org charts —
contractor prequalification, permit and submittal review, invoice and progress-claim
verification, HSE incident triage. **Every award generates a mountain of document
workflow.**

**Pitch.** *"AI contractor-submittal review department"* — agents that read technical
submittals and RFIs against spec, flag non-compliance, and route to the right engineer.
Extremely measurable: days-to-review.

**Buyer.** Chief Digital Officer · Head of PMO Digital · Head of Innovation at each entity.

**Deal shape.** Pilot on one package, then replicate — **these organisations copy each
other constantly**, so one reference sells the next four. **NHC is the warmest**: NHC
Innovation anchored LEAP East, so it is already inside the LEAP family.

## What to actually do this week

- **Elm and HUMAIN are Day 1, first three hours.** Senior people attend the opening and
  then delegate.
- **SDAIA, DGA, SITE: relationship only.** Say out loud that you know you cannot transact
  without a CR and Etimad registration, and that you are here to understand the reference
  architecture. It lands well.
- **Giga-projects: collect names, not meetings.** Their LEAP presence is usually brand
  and recruitment.
