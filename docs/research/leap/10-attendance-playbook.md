# The 48-hour playbook

**Today is Saturday 29 August 2026. LEAP opens Monday 31 August and runs to Thursday
3 September.**

The Saudi weekend is **Friday–Saturday**, so **Sunday 30 August is a normal working day in
Riyadh** — your outbound will be read. And the show runs a full Saudi working week with no
weekend interruption. That is the good news.

---

## ⚠ Verify these five things yourself before you pay for anything

The official site was unreachable when this dossier was compiled, so these came from press
coverage and secondary sources. **All five are cheap to check and expensive to get wrong:**

1. **Pass prices.** Sources conflict — General Admission is reported both as **free
   (stc-sponsored)** and as **$30 early / $50 standard**.
2. **Show opening hours.** Reported as **13:00–21:00 daily** (12:00 for some pass holders).
   *Medium confidence — and it restructures your entire day if true.*
3. **Whether LEAP registration offers an expedited event visa route.** Check the FAQ today.
4. **Your own passport's eVisa eligibility.**
5. **The exhibitor list and stand numbers** — via **`leap.invisual.app`**.

## Hour 0–2 · The visa. Nothing else matters if this fails.

- **Saudi eVisa** is available to citizens of **~66 countries** plus all GCC residents.
  **~SAR 535 (~$143)** including mandatory medical insurance. **Most applications are
  processed within 24 hours**; some sources say 2–5 business days. One-year multiple entry,
  90-day stays.
- **Visa on Arrival** for the same ~66 nationalities at major airports: **SAR 480 (~$128)**,
  card payment at immigration, **5–15 minutes**. Also available to travellers of *any*
  nationality holding a valid, previously-used **US, UK or Schengen visa**, or US/UK/EU
  permanent residency, or flying a Saudi national carrier.
- **Passport must be valid 6+ months.**
- **⚠ Red flag:** temporary restrictions periodically apply to ~14 nationalities — India,
  Pakistan, Bangladesh and Egypt are cited — for visit and business visas around Hajj
  periods. Hajj 2026 has passed, so late August *should* be clear. **That is inference.
  Verify your specific passport on the official portal today.**

> **If your passport is not eVisa or VoA eligible, you are not getting to LEAP 2026.**
> Stop here and pivot the plan to remote outreach, the LEAP East 2027 programme, and a Q4
> Riyadh trip.

## Sunday 30 August · The outbound sprint

**Channel priority in Saudi Arabia: WhatsApp > LinkedIn DM > email.** WhatsApp is the
dominant business channel and gets answered evenings and weekends. **Cold email to a Saudi
enterprise inbox with 48 hours' notice is close to useless.**

**Morning: build the person list.** LinkedIn Sales Navigator across your target companies,
in this title priority:

1. **Chief Digital Officer / Chief Digital Transformation Officer** — the most common Saudi
   enterprise AI budget holder
2. **Head of AI / Director, AI Center of Excellence**
3. **Chief Data & Analytics Officer**
4. **GM / VP Digital Transformation**
5. **Head of Innovation** — smaller budget, easiest first meeting, good internal sponsor
6. **Head of Alliances / Ecosystem** — for channel targets
7. **CTO / VP Engineering** — for fintech, often the actual decision-maker

Filter for anyone posting about LEAP this week. **They will reply.**

**Afternoon: send 60–80 messages.** Templates in `11-outreach-templates.md`. Expect 10–15%
response and **6–10 meetings**. That is a good show.

**Send 20 warm-intro requests, not 5.** In an ecosystem where a personal introduction is
close to a precondition for doing business, one warm intro is worth roughly ten cold DMs.

## Timing within the day

- **First 60–90 minutes after opening** — staff fresh, floor thin. Best for booths.
- **Last 90 minutes before close** — guards down. Best for real conversations.
- **Avoid the mid-afternoon crush** for anything needing 15+ minutes.
- **If the 13:00–21:00 hours are correct, your mornings are free** — use 09:00–12:00 for
  off-site meetings, coffee with investors, and follow-up writing.

### Prayer times — get this right

Saudi counterparts do not let meetings encroach on prayer. Across a 13:00–21:00 show day
you will hit **Asr**, **Maghrib** and **Isha**. **Never schedule across a prayer window** —
you will get five minutes and a polite exit. Look up Riyadh prayer times for 31 Aug–3 Sep
and build your calendar around them. **Doing this correctly is itself a credibility
signal.**

### Evening is where deals happen

Saudi business culture expects at least one social dinner — **typically starting after
21:00** — before contract discussions. With a 21:00 show close, **dinner at 21:30–23:00 is
the actual business meeting.** Budget for three. Asking *"are you free for dinner
Tuesday?"* on the floor is normal and welcome in Riyadh.

**Finding the side events:** there is **no published list** — they exist but aren't
indexed. Search LinkedIn and Luma for "LEAP 2026 Riyadh" filtered to the last seven days.
Country trade missions (UK, Singapore, India, Korea, France, Germany) run delegation
dinners and will often admit a citizen who emails the trade office. And ask every person
you meet on Day 1: **"what's on tonight?"** — genuinely the highest-yield question at any
tech conference.

## Practical

- **Dress** business formal; suit without a tie is fine. 40°C+ outside, heavily
  air-conditioned inside.
- **Business cards** with **Arabic on the reverse**, exchanged with the **right hand**.
- **200 printed bilingual one-pagers.** Everyone has a QR code; almost nobody has paper.
  See `01-product-profile.md`.
- **A demo that runs offline.** 93% of LEAP 2026 sessions feature live demos — the event is
  calibrated for working software. **Conference wifi at a 200,000-person event will fail
  you.** Rehearse offline; record a 60-second screen capture as backup.
- **Badge scans are not leads.** A scan puts you in a marketing nurture database. **Target
  15–25 real WhatsApp numbers with an agreed next step**, not 200 scans.

## ⛔ What not to waste time on

1. **Chasing a booth or a Rocket Fuel slot.** Both closed. It will cost you a day.
2. **Chasing a *deal* with SDAIA, DGA or any ministry.** You cannot transact without a CR,
   Etimad registration and usually an RHQ. Meet them for relationship and reference
   architecture only — **and say that's what you want.** They will respect it.
3. **Positioning against HUMAIN ONE.** See `03-targets-government.md`.
4. **Setting up an entity, hiring or signing a lease this week.** All doable remotely later.
5. **Collecting badge scans.**
6. **Leading with "AI employees replace headcount."** See `07-objections-and-context.md`.
7. **Spending Day 1 in the Startup Hub.** Your peers are there. Your buyers are at the
   enterprise stands.
8. **Trying to meet Lisa Su or Tareq Amin.** They are surrounded by handlers. **Meet their
   VP of Product or Head of Partnerships instead** — that person signs partnership
   agreements and is standing at the stand, unbothered.
9. **Keynote halls generally.** Every hour in a keynote is an hour not in LEAP Connect.
   Watch the recordings.
10. **Cold email.** WhatsApp and LinkedIn, in that order.
11. **The $14.3 trillion / $14.9 billion figures.** Infrastructure capex. Anyone quoting
    them at you is doing PR, not investing.
12. **Trying to be everywhere.** 200,000 people. Pick five targets, get them, then improvise.
