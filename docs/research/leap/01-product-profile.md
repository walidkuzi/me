## The positioning problem you must solve first

Read `07-objections-and-context.md` before writing anything here. Two constraints shape
your entire message in Saudi Arabia:

1. **HUMAIN ONE already exists.** PIF's national AI champion launched an "agentic AI
   operating system" in October 2025 that unifies HR, finance and procurement behind a
   language interface, running on ALLaM. **Do not pitch yourself as the agentic OS for
   Saudi Arabia — that slot belongs to the sovereign champion,** and its CEO is on the
   LEAP main stage this week.
2. **"AI employees replacing headcount" is politically wrong here.** Saudi Arabia's
   central economic project is *creating* jobs for Saudi nationals. The Nitaqat
   Saudisation programme entered a tougher three-year cycle in 2026, with a plan to
   localise 340,000+ additional private-sector jobs.

So the product name that works everywhere else is the one that will hurt you in Riyadh.
**Keep the product; change the sentence.**

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "AI employees that replace headcount" | "AI teammates that absorb the repetitive 60% so your Saudi hires do the 40% that needs judgement" |
| "We're the agentic OS" | "We build the vertical AI departments that run *on* HUMAIN ONE and ALLaM" |
| "Cheaper than hiring" | "More output per Saudi employee — which helps you hit your Nitaqat quota, not dodge it" |
