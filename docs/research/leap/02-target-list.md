## Tiering

**Tier A — must hit.** Seven entries, and they are not evenly weighted. Numbers 1, 2 and
6 are **channel multipliers**: each one, if it works, is worth more than every direct sale
on this list combined, because they carry the Etimad registration, the commercial
registration, the local-content score and the security clearances you do not have.
Number 7 is the opposite bet — the only targets who can sign you **this quarter**.

**Tier B — hit if time.** Real budget, slower clock, or a prime contractor already in
the way (IBM at Riyadh Air, Dyna.Ai at Ejada).

**Tier C — opportunistic.** Large ceilings, long sales cycles. Worth a stand visit and a
card, not a scheduled meeting.

## If you only get five real conversations

1. **Elm** — the channel to Saudi government. Nothing else on this list opens as many doors.
2. **stc / solutions by stc** — 75 strategic AI agreements signed in 2025, and they need
   applications to sell on top of the compute they are building.
3. **Riyad Bank** — the only Saudi bank with a named AI institution that has *publicly
   said in print* that agentic AI is its next horizon. They have told you they are shopping.
4. **HUMAIN** — not a sale. The relationship that makes every other Saudi conversation easier.
5. **Deloitte** — the most concrete agentic assets in the region and an actively staffing
   Riyadh bench that needs product to deliver.

*Ranking is inference, weighted for reachability at the show and speed to a first
paid engagement.*
